## 1. Individual access

I understand and agree that:

- my Florence account is for me only;
- I must not share my password, authenticator code or signing PIN;
- I must use my own authorised email account and authenticator;
- I must not allow another person to use Florence while signed in as me;
- actions recorded under my account may be treated as actions performed by me;
- I will immediately report an unexpected login, MFA prompt or access result.

## 2. Participant information

I will access participant information only:

- where I am assigned or rostered to support the participant;
- for a legitimate work purpose;
- to the minimum extent required to perform my role;
- in accordance with the participant's consent, support documents and I-Care Connect policy.

I will not search for, read, discuss, photograph, copy, download, export or share information about a participant I am not authorised to support.

## 3. Confidentiality and privacy

I understand that Florence may contain restricted personal and sensitive information, including health, disability, medication, support, incident, complaint, financial and identity information.

I will:

- keep participant and worker information confidential;
- avoid viewing Florence where another person can see the screen;
- use respectful and factual language in records;
- verify the correct participant before entering or signing information;
- lock my device whenever it is unattended;
- sign out on a shared or borrowed device;
- follow the Privacy and Information Management Policy and Worker Declarations.

## 4. Device security

I will use Florence only on an approved device that has:

- a PIN, password or biometric screen lock;
- current operating-system and browser updates;
- no known malware or unauthorised remote-access software;
- no shared user profile where another person can access Florence data;
- the approved Florence address or installed PWA/bookmark.

I will report a lost, stolen, compromised or replaced device immediately.

## 5. Screenshots, downloads and personal storage

Unless specifically authorised in writing for a necessary business process, I will not:

- take screenshots or screen recordings of participant, MAR, medication, progress-note, incident or staff information;
- save private Florence PDFs or participant documents to my personal phone, computer or cloud account;
- send Florence information through personal email, SMS, social media or messaging apps;
- print participant records at home;
- copy information into personal notes or calendars;
- upload Florence information to an AI service or other unapproved website.

Where an approved document must be downloaded or printed, I will follow the approved storage, access and destruction process.

## 6. Accurate and timely records

I will:

- record information during or as soon as practicable after the relevant support or event;
- distinguish fact, direct observation and participant statements from opinion;
- use the participant's own words where appropriate;
- never invent, backdate or falsify information;
- not alter a signed MAR or progress note;
- report an error and create an approved correction/follow-up record;
- complete required handover, incident, safeguarding and escalation processes.

## 7. Medication and signing PIN

I understand that my six-digit signing PIN is a digital signature.

I will:

- keep it secret;
- enter it only into the genuine Florence application;
- never sign for medication I did not personally administer, witness or record according to policy;
- record the correct outcome and reason for refused, withheld or missed medication;
- immediately report medication errors, unavailable medication, incorrect records or suspected PIN misuse.

## 8. Portal and family information

I will not treat a family or portal account as authority to disclose all participant information. I will check the participant's consent and the person's recorded authority before sharing information outside Florence.

## 9. Incidents and security concerns

I will immediately report:

- access to the wrong participant;
- information shown to an unauthorised person;
- a suspicious login or phishing message;
- a lost device;
- a shared or exposed password, MFA code or signing PIN;
- unexpected deletion or alteration;
- a public or broken document link;
- any privacy, safeguarding or medication concern.

I will preserve evidence and follow management instructions. I will not attempt to conceal an error or independently investigate a serious security or safeguarding event.

## 10. Access changes and end of engagement

I understand that I-Care Connect may limit, suspend or deactivate my access when:

- my role or participant assignment changes;
- screening, training or competency is incomplete or expired;
- I am on extended leave or suspended;
- a device or account may be compromised;
- my employment or engagement ends;
- access is no longer required.

When access ends, I will return or securely delete any authorised copies according to management instructions and will continue to protect confidentiality.
