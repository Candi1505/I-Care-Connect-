## Why you are receiving this information

I-Care Connect uses a secure internal system called **Florence** to help us organise and record the supports we provide. This addendum explains how Florence is used. It should be read with the Privacy Consent Form, Privacy and Information Management Policy and Service Agreement.

Florence does not replace your rights. You may ask questions, access or correct your information, change your consent where the law allows, make a complaint or ask for an advocate.

## What Florence may hold

Only information reasonably needed to provide, manage, safeguard, review or account for your supports should be entered. This may include:

- your name, preferred name, contact details and NDIS number;
- communication needs, preferences, goals and support information;
- health, disability, allergy and emergency information relevant to support;
- medication profiles and medication administration records where applicable;
- progress notes and participant choices;
- support plans, risk assessments and emergency plans;
- rosters, service records, incidents, complaints and follow-up actions;
- consent and authorised representative information;
- documents needed for service delivery, safeguarding, auditing or legal obligations;
- portal messages or requests you or your authorised representative send.

## Where the information is stored

Florence's primary database and private document Storage are held in the I-Care Connect Supabase project located in **Sydney, Australia (`ap-southeast-2`)**.

Florence is accessed through an approved secure web address using HTTPS. The application may use other approved providers for website delivery, email invitations and optional accounting functions. I-Care Connect will limit the information sent to those providers to what is necessary for the relevant purpose.

Xero, if used, receives approved invoice/accounting information only. Clinical notes, medication administration records, support plans and incident details must not be sent to Xero.

## Who can access information

Access is based on role and need:

- **Supervisors** can manage service and governance information needed for their responsibilities.
- **Support workers** can access only the participant information they are assigned or rostered to use for support.
- **Family representative portal users** can use a secure portal linked to the participant where the participant has authorised that access.
- **Participant portal users** can use their own secure portal.

Family and participant portal users do not receive raw staff medication records, MAR, progress notes, incidents, staff files or governance records through the portal.

Every user has an individual account, password and authenticator verification. Support workers and supervisors also use their own signing PIN for MAR and progress notes. Passwords, authenticator codes and signing PINs must not be shared.

## Security and records

I-Care Connect uses controls including:

- individual invite-only accounts;
- authenticator MFA;
- participant-specific access limits;
- private document Storage and expiring document links;
- audit records of changes and selected access/download/export events;
- encrypted exports;
- database and private document backups;
- incident and data-breach response procedures;
- access review and staff training.

No online system can be guaranteed to have zero risk. I-Care Connect assesses and manages risks, monitors access, responds to incidents and maintains fallback arrangements for outages.

## Your choices and rights

You may:

- ask what information is held about you;
- request access to or correction of your information;
- ask how your information is used or shared;
- change or withdraw consent for optional uses, subject to legal and service-record obligations;
- nominate or remove an authorised representative or advocate;
- ask for information in an accessible format;
- make a complaint without fear of adverse treatment;
- contact external complaint or advocacy services.

Some records may need to be kept even after consent changes or services end because of legal, incident, financial, employment, audit or safeguarding obligations. I-Care Connect will restrict and dispose of records according to its retention and information-management processes.
